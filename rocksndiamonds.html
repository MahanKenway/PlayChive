Fully bundled rock-and-gem game: Rocks’n’Diamonds is compiled from a pinned official source revision using its maintained Emscripten target. The package includes its own levels, graphics, sounds and music in a local data bundle, and requires no upload or external playable data. The only packaging source change is an Emscripten guard around a Windows-only SDL DPI hint. GPL-2.0-or-later · Source notice.
