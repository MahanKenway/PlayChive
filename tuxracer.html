About TuxRacer.js

A complete existing open-source 3D downhill racer, bundled from the TuxRacer.js project with its penguin, snow courses, music and sound. Race for the finish, collect herring and switch among twenty local courses with keyboard, the game’s touch joystick, PLAYCHIVE PSP controls, gamepad support and fullscreen—no upload, account or external game data required.

TuxRacer.js: frequently asked questions

Is TuxRacer.js free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. TuxRacer.js runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (GPL-2.0-only code and retained upstream credits / source). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play TuxRacer.js?

No. TuxRacer.js runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play TuxRacer.js on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes TuxRacer.js and what is its license?

TuxRacer.js is developed by Jan Ebbe / Extreme Tux Racer & Tux Racer contributors. License and attribution: GPL-2.0-only code and retained upstream credits / source. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
