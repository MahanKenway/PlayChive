## PLAYCHIVE: free classic and open-source games in your browser

PLAYCHIVE is a curated hub of 23 playable browser runtimes plus a rights-aware directory of 34 further catalog entries. Each game runs as WebAssembly or HTML5 in your browser: no install, no account, and saves stay on your device. PLAYCHIVE is non-commercial and keeps upstream license notices with every runtime. ## Frequently asked questions

### What is PLAYCHIVE?

PLAYCHIVE is a curated browser hub for classic, open-source and freeware games. Independent WebAssembly and HTML5 runtimes let you play them directly in the browser, with no install and no account.

### Are the games free and do I need to install anything?

Games marked Ready or Free / bundled are free and include the content they need. Nothing has to be installed and no account is required. ECWolf is the exception: it ships only the engine, so you select your own lawful data files.

### Does PLAYCHIVE work on phones and with controllers?

Yes. Supported runtimes offer PSP-style touch controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a gamepad. Fullscreen is available from each runtime page.

### Where are my saves stored?

Progress is kept in your own browser storage, per game. It never goes to a PLAYCHIVE server, so clearing site data for the domain also clears your saves.

### Does PLAYCHIVE include commercial games such as the original Doom?

No. PLAYCHIVE does not distribute commercial game data. It bundles free content such as Freedoom, and a separate local importer lets you load WAD files you own; they stay in your browser session.
