## About Neverball

A complete 3D physics-puzzle game in its own Neverball WebAssembly runtime. It bundles the libre base game and Easy level set, keeps saves in the browser, includes PSP-style touch controls and never asks the player to upload data or download a campaign.

Yes. Neverball runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (GPLv2-or-later engine and base data · notices retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

### Do I need to install anything to play Neverball?

No. Neverball runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.
