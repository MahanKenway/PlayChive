## About OpenTTD

A complete transport-strategy game in its own OpenTTD WebAssembly runtime. It bundles the free OpenGFX graphics, OpenSFX effects and OpenMSX music sets, keeps saves in the browser, includes PSP-style touch controls and never asks the player to upload Transport Tycoon Deluxe files.

### OpenTTD: frequently asked questions

### Is OpenTTD free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. OpenTTD runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (GPL-2.0 engine + OpenGFX/OpenMSX · OpenSFX attribution notice retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

### Do I need to install anything to play OpenTTD?

No. OpenTTD runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

### Can I play OpenTTD on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

### Who makes OpenTTD and what is its license?

OpenTTD is developed by OpenTTD project / OpenGFX / OpenSFX / OpenMSX. License and attribution: GPL-2.0 engine + OpenGFX/OpenMSX · OpenSFX attribution notice retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
