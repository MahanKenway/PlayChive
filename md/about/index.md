# About, imprint and contact

**Last updated: 29 October 2026.** PLAYCHIVE is a free, non-commercial browser game hub maintained by Mahan Tavakoli (GitHub: MahanKenway), based in Tehran, Iran.

## Imprint

- Operator: Mahan Tavakoli, independent developer
- Website: [playchive.ir](https://playchive.ir/)
- Source code: [github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive](https://github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive)
- Business model: none. No ads, accounts, payments or tracking.

## Contact

For questions, rights or takedown requests about a game or asset, open an issue at [github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive/issues](https://github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive/issues) or write to the maintainer through [GitHub / MahanKenway](https://github.com/MahanKenway).

## Rights and licensing

Games, engines and archives remain the property of their creators. Each runtime keeps its upstream licence notices, and commercial game data is never distributed. See the [catalog](https://playchive.ir/) for per-game licences and the [privacy policy](https://playchive.ir/privacy/) for data handling.
