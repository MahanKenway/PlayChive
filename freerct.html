Fully bundled libre game: FreeRCT is a clean-room, GPL-2.0-only theme-park strategy game. This runtime compiles its upstream WebAssembly path and bundles the project’s own generated game data. It does not include RollerCoaster Tycoon or OpenRCT2 data, and it never asks the player to upload files. Saves stay in browser-local storage. License · Source and attribution.
