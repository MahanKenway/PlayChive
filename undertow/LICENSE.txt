Undertow

No license text accompanies the original single-file game. PLAYCHIVE publishes
it with the author's permission, as confirmed by the PLAYCHIVE owner (Mahan
Kenway) on 2026-11-18. If the author asks, PLAYCHIVE removes it.

Original source artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/2UXyB49DrVHFfhTMdNDFTA
The game is built on the engine and visual language of Saltline (see
../saltline/LICENSE.txt). The bundled three.js library is MIT licensed
(Copyright 2010-2024 Three.js Authors).

Changes by PLAYCHIVE and Mahan Kenway:
- The Google Fonts stylesheet was replaced by self-hosted OFL fonts (Big
  Shoulders Display, IBM Plex Mono, Barlow Condensed; licenses in
  undertow/fonts/). Persian text uses Shabnam and Vazirmatn (OFL) from
  assets/fonts.
- Persian edition by Mahan Kenway: undertow/fa.js translates menus, HUD, radio
  lines and prompts when PLAYCHIVE's saved language is Persian. The game script
  got two one-line hooks (objective and radio text), a credit line on the
  title screen and Persian-only CSS. English is unchanged.
