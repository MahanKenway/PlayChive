Pacman: frequently asked questions

Is Pacman free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Pacman runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (MIT · attribution retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Pacman?

No. Pacman runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Pacman on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes Pacman and what is its license?

Pacman is developed by Haole Zheng / mumuy. License and attribution: MIT · attribution retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
