Obsidian Relay: frequently asked questions

Is Obsidian Relay free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Obsidian Relay runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (PLAYCHIVE original gameplay and assets · OpenResident adapter source retained under BSD-2-Clause · non-commercial platform deployment). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Obsidian Relay?

No. Obsidian Relay runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Obsidian Relay on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.
