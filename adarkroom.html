A Dark Room: frequently asked questions

Is A Dark Room free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. A Dark Room runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (MPL-2.0 · source and notices retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play A Dark Room?

No. A Dark Room runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play A Dark Room on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes A Dark Room and what is its license?

A Dark Room is developed by Michael Townsend & contributors. License and attribution: MPL-2.0 · source and notices retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
