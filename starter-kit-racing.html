Fully bundled 3D racing game: Starter Kit Racing is packaged from a pinned MIT-licensed upstream snapshot by mrdoob. The track, forest, tents and truck glTF meshes are supplied upstream as Kenney CC0 assets. PLAYCHIVE vendors the browser modules locally and adds PSP touch controls, gamepad support and fullscreen only. MIT License · Upstream README · Source notice.
