Starter Kit Racing: frequently asked questions

Is Starter Kit Racing free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Starter Kit Racing runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (MIT upstream source · Kenney CC0 glTF models · notices retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Starter Kit Racing?

No. Starter Kit Racing runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Starter Kit Racing on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes Starter Kit Racing and what is its license?

Starter Kit Racing is developed by mrdoob / Kenney. License and attribution: MIT upstream source · Kenney CC0 glTF models · notices retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
