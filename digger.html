Digger Remastered: frequently asked questions

Is Digger Remastered free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Digger Remastered runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (Historical mixed upstream notices retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Digger Remastered?

No. Digger Remastered runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Digger Remastered on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes Digger Remastered and what is its license?

Digger Remastered is developed by Digger Remastered contributors. License and attribution: Historical mixed upstream notices retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
