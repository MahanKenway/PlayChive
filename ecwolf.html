ECWolf: frequently asked questions

Is ECWolf free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

The ECWolf engine is free and runs in your browser, but PLAYCHIVE does not distribute the original game data. You select your own lawfully obtained files locally and they stay in your browser.

Do I need to install anything to play ECWolf?

No. ECWolf runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play ECWolf on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes ECWolf and what is its license?

ECWolf is developed by ECWolf project / ECWolf JS. License and attribution: GPL engine resources · original game data not distributed. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
