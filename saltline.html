About Saltline

A browser first-person shooter set in a rainy container port. Cut three uplink relays while drones and robot dogs hunt you. Models, sounds and textures are generated in code, so the whole game runs from one local page with keyboard and mouse, no upload, account or external game data.

Saltline: frequently asked questions

Is Saltline free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Saltline runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (MIT · credit and notice retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Saltline?

No. Saltline runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Saltline on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes Saltline and what is its license?

Saltline is developed by Claude Fable 5.2 (run set up by Felix Rieseberg). License and attribution: MIT · credit and notice retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
