2048: frequently asked questions

Is 2048 free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. 2048 runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (MIT · source and notices retained). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play 2048?

No. 2048 runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play 2048 on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes 2048 and what is its license?

2048 is developed by Gabriele Cirulli & contributors. License and attribution: MIT · source and notices retained. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
