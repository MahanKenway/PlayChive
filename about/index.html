About, imprint and contact

Last updated: 29 October 2026. PLAYCHIVE is a free, non-commercial browser game hub maintained by Mahan Tavakoli (GitHub: MahanKenway), based in Tehran, Iran.

Imprint

  • Operator: Mahan Tavakoli, independent developer
  • Website: playchive.ir
  • Source code: github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive
  • Business model: none. No ads, accounts, payments or tracking.

Contact

For questions, rights or takedown requests about a game or asset, open an issue at github.com/MahanKenway/PlayChive/issues or write to the maintainer through GitHub / MahanKenway.

Rights and licensing

Games, engines and archives remain the property of their creators. Each runtime keeps its upstream licence notices, and commercial game data is never distributed. See the catalog for per-game licences and the privacy policy for data handling.
