Armawebtron: frequently asked questions

Is Armawebtron free to play on PLAYCHIVE?

Yes. Armawebtron runs free in your browser. PLAYCHIVE bundles the engine and the game content it needs, distributed under their own open or freeware terms (GPL-2.0-or-later code and retained upstream source / assets). There is no sign-up and no file upload step.

Do I need to install anything to play Armawebtron?

No. Armawebtron runs directly in a modern desktop or mobile browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari, using WebAssembly or HTML5. No account is required.

Can I play Armawebtron on a phone or with a controller?

PLAYCHIVE supports keyboard, on-screen touch controls, gamepad and fullscreen mode. Where the runtime allows it, phones get PSP-style on-screen controls, and desktop browsers accept keyboard, mouse and a connected gamepad.

Who makes Armawebtron and what is its license?

Armawebtron is developed by Glen Harpring / Armawebtron contributors. License and attribution: GPL-2.0-or-later code and retained upstream source / assets. PLAYCHIVE is a non-commercial hub and keeps upstream notices with each runtime.
